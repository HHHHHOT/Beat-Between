# Beat Between ## Introduction **Beat Between** is an original manga about dance, an idol group, and a young choreographer. The story follows 23-year-old choreographer **Aya** and the four-member male group **NOVA**. Aya is serious, strict, and highly professional at work. But after work, she is cheerful, natural, funny, and loves to complain about everyday life. --- # 📖 Read Online ## Chapters 1 + 2 Complete Edition [▶️ Read Beat Between Online](https://hhhhhot.github.io/Beat-Between/index.pdf) --- # Chapter 01 ## Teacher, Do You Really Understand Us? Aya begins her first official choreography session with NOVA. During the first rehearsal, she quickly notices the individual habits of all four members. The person who catches her attention the most is NOVA's rapper— **Ryusei.** He rarely makes mistakes. But he focuses too much on hitting every beat perfectly. Before his body finishes the current beat, his mind is already moving toward the next one. Aya tells him: > Your mind reaches the next beat before your body does. Then she asks him directly: > Are you afraid people won't notice you? For the first time, her words hit something deeper inside him. After rehearsal, Ryusei sends Aya a message: > Teacher. > I want to be the first one here tomorrow. > I'm still not satisfied with that part today. Aya replies: > Then don't be late. The first rehearsal ends. And for the first time, he wants someone to truly see his dance. --- # Chapter 02 ## Teacher Is Off Duty After rehearsal, Aya leaves BEAT ZONE. The moment she steps outside, her serious teacher mode disappears. Then she suddenly realizes— she never ate dinner. A convenience store. Rice balls. Fried chicken. Pudding. Potato chips. The strict professional choreographer is, after all, just a normal 23-year-old woman after work. Later, her friend calls and asks about her first day working with NOVA. Eventually, her friend asks: > So, which one is the most troublesome? Aya answers immediately: > Ryusei. There is a short silence. > ...You remembered his name pretty quickly. Aya immediately denies any hidden meaning. > Because he's the most troublesome. > It has nothing to do with his name. But on the way home, she keeps thinking about his dancing. Finally, she writes in her notebook: > Let him dare to stay between the beats. Back home, alone in her apartment, she showers, eats pudding, and watches television. After her first day with NOVA, Aya comes to one conclusion: **NOVA is more interesting than I expected.** **Especially him.** --- # Characters ## Aya - 23 years old - Choreographer - Serious and strict at work - Highly sensitive to rhythm, balance, movement, and positioning - Cheerful and relaxed after work - Loves making sarcastic comments - Lives alone --- ## Ryusei - 21 years old - NOVA's rapper - Debuted about one year ago - Fast learner - Strong sense of rhythm - Solid dance fundamentals - Competitive personality - Focuses too much on hitting every beat - Deeply wants to be noticed --- # NOVA A four-member male idol group.
